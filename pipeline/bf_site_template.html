How each number is counted
Texts sentOutbound texts on the Black Friday SMS Blast line.
DeliveredTexts sent minus failed ones (landlines, unreachable phones).
RepliesPeople who texted back on that line, counted once each in the date range.
YESPeople whose reply carries Aloware's Black Friday YES tag. A support question can land here.
YES replies (charts)Trend and by-hour charts count YES texts, so one person can count more than once.
ConnectedPeople with at least one call of 90 seconds or more on the Black Friday line.
ConvertedOrders in the call tracker with Call Type "SMS blast", one per rep and customer per day.
What soldBFCM bundles counted on their own. Products with "Trial" in the name are trials, not paid PG1.
By groupPast buyers = the main blast list. Failed Close = the HubSpot Failed Close (Oct–Nov) list plus 15 past buyers from it.
DatesEastern time. Weeks start on Sunday.
Response timeFirst YES text → first outbound call on the Black Friday line after it. "Not called yet" = no call so far.
Call outcomesGrouped by the phone team's disposition categories (same sheet as the TMA report). Calls with no disposition are grouped by how the call ended.
Avg callback (gauge)Average minutes from a YES text sent 8 AM–5 PM ET to our first outbound call. Green at 5 min or less, amber up to 10, red above.
Response statusEach YES lead once: On time = called within 30 min during hours; Late = called after 30 min; Inbound completed = they called us first; Outside hoop = YES came before 8 AM or after 5 PM ET; No callback found = not called yet.
OutcomeThe category in the Call Disposition sheet. New dispositions use the suggested category until the sheet's category column is filled.
RepThe rep on the call (or the rep it rang, for unanswered preview-dialer calls). Sales are matched to reps by first and last name.
